\section{Additional Results}
\label{app:results}

\Cref{fig:spectrum} is the threshold sweep of \cref{sec:exp:rq2}.
\Cref{tab:errors}
lists the rejected citations discussed there; \cref{tab:accepted} lists the
term types the maintenance iterations added; \cref{tab:evaltag,tab:evalret}
hold the gold-set evaluation of \cref{sec:exp:rq3}: \GItems\ held-out
items were annotated twice by \texttt{\GModel} at effort high, and a (term
type, value) label is gold when both passes assert it with verified evidence
(pass agreement micro-F$_1$ \GAgreement\%, \GDropped\ labels dropped); no
human labels are involved; each system is run \EvalRunsWord\ times. \Cref{tab:billing}
is the billing of every stage. The seed catalogue, the case study,
the category trees, the remaining prompts and the full DAG are in the
released artefact.
