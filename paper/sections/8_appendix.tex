\section{Additional Results}
\label{app:results}

\Cref{fig:tree} renders the asset category of the DAG discussed in
\cref{sec:exp:rq2} and \cref{fig:spectrum} its threshold sweep; \cref{tab:objectives}
holds the per-version objectives of \cref{sec:exp:rq3}. \Cref{tab:case} is the case study of \cref{sec:exp:rq1}; \cref{tab:errors}
lists the rejected citations discussed there; \cref{tab:accepted} lists the
term types the maintenance iterations added. The full seed catalogue of 93
term types, the primary-parent trees of the other four categories, the Task B,
discovery and consolidation prompts, and the full DAG with every distribution
are in the released artefact.
