\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We proposed \sys, which induces the classification dimensions of crypto-asset
law and research from definitional text with a citation for every element,
orders them into a DAG by one cited is-a query per node, and maintains the
DAG from tagged Web content by measured information gain. On 24 documents it
produced 93 term types with 317 values, 97.9\% of whose 480 citations verify,
and \BEdges\ weighted is-a edges; two maintenance iterations over \AllItems\ DLT-Corpus
items verified 96--97\% of the tag evidence and grew the graph by \AllAddedTypes\
dimensions and \AllAddedValues\ values for \$\AllCost. Future work follows the limits of
this evidence: expert validation of whether verified citations and tags
support their nodes; repeated runs and a second model, since identifiers are
not yet stable across runs; and regulatory batches with a trained classifier
replacing the LLM tagger.
