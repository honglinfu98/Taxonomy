\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Taxonomy construction, expansion and completion.}
Most work at this venue assumes a seed taxonomy and inserts new concepts into
it. TaxoExpan~\cite{shen2020taxoexpan} and HEF~\cite{wang2021hef} attach a query
concept to a parent using self-supervision from the seed's own edges;
TaxoEnrich~\cite{jiang2022taxoenrich} and TaxoComplete~\cite{arous2023taxocomplete}
score parent--child positions; ICON~\cite{shi2024icon} generates concepts that
the seed implies but does not name; TaxoCom~\cite{lee2022taxocom} discovers
new topic clusters under a partial hierarchy; ToTER~\cite{kang2024toter} uses
a corpus taxonomy to improve retrieval. Taxonomies built from scratch~\cite{chu2019tifi,redi2019citation}
decompose the task into stages with their own gold sets. Our setting differs in two ways. There is no seed, and the nodes
we induce are dimensions of classification rather than leaf concepts, each
anchored in a cited passage.

\paragraph{LLMs for classification, tagging and ontology learning.}
TELEClass~\cite{zhang2025teleclass} enriches a given taxonomy with LLM-mined
class features; TnT-LLM~\cite{wan2024tntllm} generates a label taxonomy from
text at scale; TagGPT~\cite{li2023taggpt} and ICXML~\cite{zhu2024icxml} tag or
classify zero-shot; LLMs4OL~\cite{babaeigiglou2023llms4ol} benchmarks term
typing and hierarchy discovery; OntoTune~\cite{liu2025ontotune} aligns an LLM to
an ontology; Graphusion~\cite{yang2025graphusion} builds a knowledge graph with
retrieval and a global fusion step. These methods either take the label space
as given or generate it without provenance, and enrichment from a corpus
\cite{zhang2025teleclass,lee2022taxocom} adds features or topics without a
criterion for when the taxonomy should change. Unlike them, we require every
induced class and value to cite a document and section, verify the citation,
and admit content-derived additions only when they raise a measured
information gain.

\paragraph{Crypto-asset taxonomies and grounding.}
Tasca and Tessone~\cite{tasca2019taxonomy} classify blockchain architectures by
component; Zhang et al.~\cite{zhang2026cryptotaxonomy} classify assets by
technical standard, centralisation, function and legal status; DLT-Corpus
\cite{hernandez2026dltcorpus} collects domain text; Avice et
al.~\cite{cryptotags2024} link attribution tags to knowledge graph entities with
an LLM. Retrieval-augmented generation~\cite{lewis2020rag} and hierarchical
retrieval~\cite{sarthi2024raptor} ground an LLM in chunks of a corpus. We
instead hold the whole definitional corpus in a cached context, because
recognising one dimension under different names across sources is a
whole-corpus judgement that chunk retrieval would have to anticipate.
