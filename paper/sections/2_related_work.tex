\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Taxonomy construction, expansion and completion.}
Taxonomy induction from text has a long lineage, from hypernym classifiers and
definition graphs~\cite{snow2006taxonomy,velardi2013ontolearn,bordea2016texeval}
to embedding-based construction~\cite{zhang2018taxogen,shen2018hiexpan,huang2020corel}.
Most work at this venue assumes a seed taxonomy and inserts new concepts into
it. TaxoExpan~\cite{shen2020taxoexpan} and HEF~\cite{wang2021hef} attach a query
concept to a parent using self-supervision from the seed's own edges;
TaxoEnrich~\cite{jiang2022taxoenrich} and TaxoComplete~\cite{arous2023taxocomplete}
score parent--child positions; ICON~\cite{shi2024icon} inserts implicit
concepts; TaxoCom~\cite{lee2022taxocom} discovers
new topic clusters under a partial hierarchy; ToTER~\cite{kang2024toter} uses
a corpus taxonomy to improve retrieval. Taxonomies built from scratch~\cite{chu2019tifi,redi2019citation}
decompose the task into stages with their own gold sets. Our work differs:
there is no seed, and the nodes we induce are classification dimensions rather
than leaf concepts, each anchored in a cited passage.

\paragraph{LLMs for classification, tagging and ontology learning.}
LLMs induce taxonomies zero-shot or from a few examples~\cite{jain2022zeroshot,
zeng2024chainoflayer,lo2024ollm}, and topic or concept
induction~\cite{pham2024topicgpt,lam2024lloom} names the categories a corpus
supports. TaxoClass~\cite{shen2021taxoclass} and TELEClass~\cite{zhang2025teleclass}
classify under a given taxonomy from class names and LLM-mined features; TnT-LLM~\cite{wan2024tntllm} generates a label taxonomy from
text at scale; TagGPT~\cite{li2023taggpt} and ICXML~\cite{zhu2024icxml} tag or
classify zero-shot; LLMs4OL~\cite{babaeigiglou2023llms4ol} benchmarks term
typing and hierarchy discovery; OntoTune~\cite{liu2025ontotune} aligns an LLM to
an ontology; Graphusion~\cite{yang2025graphusion} fuses retrieved triples into
a knowledge graph. These methods take the label space as
given or generate it without provenance, and enrichment from a
corpus~\cite{zhang2025teleclass,lee2022taxocom} has no criterion for when the
taxonomy should change. We require every class and value to cite a verified
passage and admit content-derived additions only when they raise a measured
information gain.

\paragraph{Crypto-asset taxonomies and grounding.}
Tasca and Tessone~\cite{tasca2019taxonomy} and Ballandies et
al.~\cite{ballandies2022dlt} classify ledger designs by component; Zhang et al.~\cite{zhang2026cryptotaxonomy} classify assets by
technical standard, centralisation, function and legal status; DLT-Corpus
\cite{hernandez2026dltcorpus} collects domain text; Avice et
al.~\cite{cryptotags2024} link attribution tags to knowledge graph entities with
an LLM. Attribution evaluation and citation generation~\cite{rashkin2023ais,
gao2023alce,liu2023verifiability} ask whether an output is supported by its
sources, which our verifier checks mechanically. Retrieval-augmented
generation~\cite{lewis2020rag} and hierarchical retrieval~\cite{sarthi2024raptor}
ground an LLM in chunks; we hold the whole definitional corpus in a cached
context, despite the known loss of mid-context information~\cite{liu2024lostmiddle},
because recognising one dimension under different names is a whole-corpus
judgement.
