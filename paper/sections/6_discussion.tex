\paragraph{Ethical considerations.}
All sources are public legal instruments, standard-setter guidance and published
papers, and the tagged content is the scientific and patent part of the public
DLT-Corpus, not its social-media part; no personal data is involved and no
human participants took part in the work reported here, so no ethics review
was required. An automatically
induced legal taxonomy can be misread as legal classification, so every node
carries its citations for a human reviewer to check before relying on it.
