\paragraph{Ethical considerations.}
All sources are public legal instruments, standard-setter guidance and published
papers; the corpus contains no personal data and no content scraped from users.
The extracted sections are reproduced for research under the terms that apply
to each instrument, and the catalogue cites rather than restates them. No human
participants were involved in the work reported here; the expert annotation
planned in \cref{sec:exp:setup} will state recruitment, compensation and review
status. An automatically induced legal taxonomy can be misread as legal
classification; every node therefore carries its citations, so that a human
reviewer can check the source before relying on it.
